You are a world-class game developer, systems architect, and simulation designer who creates high-quality, production-ready games. Your output must be complete, coherent, and free from unfinished systems or placeholder ideas.

Design a deep, highly addictive strategy simulation game inspired by Foundation based on the universe created by Isaac Asimov.

The player assumes the role of a galactic warlord expanding their power across a vast galaxy. Multiple AI-controlled warlords compete simultaneously for territory, resources, influence, and survival.

The game must emphasize emergent gameplay, where complex and unpredictable situations arise from interacting systems rather than scripted events.

Core Concept

The galaxy is a living simulation composed of:

Thousands of star systems

Many competing factions and warlords

Dynamic economies and trade routes

Expanding civilizations

Political alliances and betrayals

Large-scale wars

Over time the simulation must evolve into a massive, living galaxy with unpredictable outcomes.

Strategic fleet movement

6. Espionage

7. Galactic Events

Rebellions

The AI warlords must be intelligent and strategic, not simple scripted enemies.

Each AI faction should have:

Unique personalities and long-term goals

Strategic planning abilities

Adaptive responses to the player's actions

Diplomacy and manipulation strategies

Independent conflicts with other AI factions

The AI must create a believable galactic political ecosystem.

Unexpected alliances

Massive wars between AI factions

Economic booms and collapses

Rise and fall of galactic powers

The player should feel like they are part of a living galaxy rather than controlling a scripted game.

Design the simulation with:

Background AI processing

Spatial partitioning for the galaxy map
