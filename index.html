HOW TO PLAY

Core Loop

The simulation advances in turns automatically. Each turn every faction processes economy, fleet movement, diplomacy, research, and AI decisions simultaneously.

Galactic Map

Click a star system to select it. Right-click drag or scroll wheel to pan/zoom. Selected systems show details in the right panel.

Victory Conditions

  • Domination - Control 70% of star systems
  • Economic - Hold a 10,000 credit lead over rivals
  • Scientific - Research all 25 technologies
  • Diplomatic - Maintain 5 simultaneous alliances
  • Survival - Be the last faction standing

Key Systems

  • Economy: Systems yield Credits, Minerals, Food, Research, Influence each turn. Develop systems to amplify yields.
  • Trade Routes: Connected systems with active routes multiply yields by 12% per route.
  • Fleets: Build and move fleets to conquer, defend, colonise.
  • Diplomacy: Alliances, trade pacts, wars. Reputation affects all negotiations.
  • Technology: 25 techs across Military, Economy, Science, Diplomacy, Espionage branches.
  • Espionage: Intel, Sabotage, Rebellion, Assassination missions.
  • Events: Random galactic events fire each turn. Psychohistory modelling mitigates extremes.

Controls

  • Space - Pause/Unpause
  • Ctrl+S - Quick Save
  • Escape - Close modal
  • Right-click drag - Pan map
  • Scroll wheel - Zoom map
